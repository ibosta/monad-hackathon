WHAT IS MONAD · 08 

###### Async execution, simplified. 

A restaurant where the front of house never waits for the kitchen. 

MOST BLOCKCHAINS 

###### One person does both jobs. 

The waiter takes an order, walks to the kitchen, cooks it, serves it, then takes the next order. Everybody in the queue waits for the slowest dish. 

MONAD 

###### Two stations, always busy. 

Front of house keeps taking orders while the kitchen cooks the previous ones. Both run flat out, and no order is lost: the tickets are already agreed and in order. 

- 01 Taking orders = consensus. Validators agree what the transactions are, and in what order. 

- 02 Cooking = execution. Actually running those transactions and updating balances. 

- 03 Splitting them gives each job a full block of time instead of a slice: same 300ms blocks, far more work per block. 

WHAT IS MONAD · 09 

###### Parallel execution, simplified. 

Ten bank tellers on one ledger, and a redo only when two touch the same account. 

MOST BLOCKCHAINS MONAD 

###### One teller for the whole bank. 

Customers are served strictly one at a time, in case two of them touch the same account. Safe, and slow: almost every pair was independent anyway. 

###### Every window open, optimistically. 

Serve everyone at once, assuming their accounts donʼt overlap. Then check the paperwork: if two did hit the same account, only that one is redone with the corrected balance. 

- 01 Optimistic = assume no overlap, verify after. Being occasionally wrong is cheaper than everyone waiting. 

- 02 Most transactions are strangers to each other: different wallets, different apps, no overlap. 

- 03 Redone work still follows the agreed order, so the outcome is identical to serving one at a time.
